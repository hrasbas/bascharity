"YOUR PURCHASE SATISFIES A HUNGER"

BAS Charity International acts as a bridge between global commerce and humanitarian aid. Our mission is to ensure that support reaches those who truly need it through affiliate partnerships with 50,000+ online shops worldwide.

Financial Governance

Initially, 100% of the affiliate income generated through this platform was dedicated entirely to hunger relief. As we scale, a minimum of 51% or more will always be strictly dedicated to these noble causes.

The remaining portion is utilized for platform maintenance, technical development, staff support, and operational expenses to ensure long-term sustainability.

We value your digital safety. BAS Charity International does not collect or store your personal financial information. When you click the above links, your transaction happens directly on the official retailer's secure website.

Shopping through the above links costs you nothing extra. The retailers pay a commission to us, which we use for our humanitarian missions to fight hunger and help those in need.
